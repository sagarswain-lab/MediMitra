The Problem

Millions of Indians face this every single day

Healthcare in India is riddled with barriers — language, distance, and trust. MediMitra is built to break all three.

📄
700M+
Can't read their own prescriptions
Over 700 million rural Indians receive prescriptions in English — a language they don't read. Medicines go unused, misused, or dangerously replaced.
💊
1 in 4
Rural drugs are counterfeit or substandard
25% of medicines sold in rural India are fake or substandard. Without a way to verify, patients unknowingly consume harmful substances daily.
🏥
40%
Villages have no nearby doctor
In 40% of rural villages, the nearest qualified doctor is more than 5 km away. For routine health questions, most people simply go without answers.

FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

Is MediMitra really completely free?
Yes — 100% free, forever. MediMitra is a public health project. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no premium tiers. Every feature is free for every user, everywhere.
Do I need to create an account to use it?
No. You can use all 7 core features without signing in at all. We offer optional Google Sign-In only if you want AI personalization based on your health profile — allergies, chronic conditions, medications. Without sign-in, everything works — your history is stored locally on your device. Sign in to unlock personalized AI responses across all features.
What does the Health Profile actually do?
When you sign in and save your health profile (allergies, chronic conditions, current medications, blood group, height/weight), every AI feature automatically uses this data. The Symptom Checker warns if your condition worsens the symptoms. The Prescription Reader flags medicines you may be allergic to. The Lifestyle Advisor never suggests foods you're allergic to and adapts exercise intensity to your conditions. The Medicine Scanner shows a personalized suitability rating. It's one profile — powering all 7 features.
Is my health information safe and private?
Your health queries are processed only to generate a response — they are never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles. If you don't sign in, nothing is stored on our servers. Your data stays private.
Can I really use it in Hindi or Odia?
Absolutely. MediMitra supports 7 languages: English, Hindi, Odia, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and Marathi. Just select your language from the dropdown — every AI response, including symptom analysis, drug explanations, and lifestyle advice, will be in that language.
Is this a replacement for a real doctor?
No — and we are very transparent about this. MediMitra is an AI-assisted health information tool. It helps you understand symptoms, read prescriptions, and make more informed decisions. It does not diagnose medical conditions. Always consult a licensed doctor for diagnosis and treatment.
Who built MediMitra?
MediMitra was built by Sagar Swain as a public health initiative to make intelligent healthcare guidance accessible to everyone.
